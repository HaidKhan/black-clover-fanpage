1. Elf Reincarnation Saga (Sub-Arcs)

The overarching saga following Asta's journey from receiving his 5-Leaf Grimoire to uncovering the truth behind the Eye of the Midnight Sun and the ancient Elf Tribe.

Sub-Arc Episodes Manga Chapters Key Focus
Magic Knights Entrance Arc 1 – 13 1 – 10 Asta and Yuno receive their grimoires and join the Black Bulls and Golden Dawn.
Dungeon Exploration Arc 14 – 19 11 – 21 Asta obtains the Demon-Dweller Sword and clashes with Mars of the Diamond Kingdom.
Royal Capital Assault Arc 20 – 27 22 – 37 The Eye of the Midnight Sun attacks the Clover Capital; Fuegoleon is ambushed.
Eye of the Midnight Sun Encounter Arc 28 – 39 38 – 56 Yami battles Licht in a cave, unveiling Light Magic vs. Dark Magic.
Seabed Temple Arc 40 – 50 57 – 74 The Black Bulls work together to defeat Vetto of the Third Eye and claim a magic stone.
Witches' Forest Arc 51 – 65 75 – 101 Vanessa awakens the Red Thread of Fate and Asta unlocks his Black Form against Ladros.

2. Heart Kingdom & Spade Kingdom Saga

After learning of the Devil Megicula and the Dark Triad, the Clover Kingdom forms an alliance with the Heart Kingdom to take the fight directly to Spade.

Heart Kingdom Joint Struggle Arc

Episodes: 130 – 157
Chapters: 229 – 260

Asta and the Magic Knights train in the Heart Kingdom, mastering Mana Method and True Magic before the Dark Triad invades both Heart and Golden Dawn.

Spade Kingdom Raid Arc

Episodes: 158 – 170+
Chapters: 261 – 331

Asta masters Devil Union with Liebe as the Clover resistance storms Spade Castle to rescue Yami and Vangeance from Dante, Vanica, Zenon, and Lucifero.

Ultimate Wizard King (Final) Arc

Status: Manga Ongoing
Chapters: 332 – Present

Lucius Zogratis reveals his true identity and declares Judgment Day, forcing Asta to train in the Land of the Sun before the final war for the Clover Kingdom.
