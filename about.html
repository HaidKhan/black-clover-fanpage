About the Series

Black Clover is a Japanese fantasy shonen manga series written and illustrated by Yuuki Tabata. Serialized starting in 2015 and adapted into a 170-episode anime by Studio Pierrot, the story follows Asta—a boy born without a drop of magic in a world where magic is everything—and his rival Yuno as they strive to become the Wizard King.
